HollyHock3 is installed as a mod for your ClassPad. The current installer is made for Windows only.

If you use macOS, follow the [Install on macOS](/wiki/hh3/getting-started/install-mac/) guide. If you use Linux, we recommend using a Windows virtual machine. Wine should work too, but if you run into issues, please [join our Discord server](https://discord.gg/knpcNJTzpd) and tell us what happened.

## Install the mod

Go to [classpad.dev](https://classpad.dev), then run the installer command from PowerShell:

1. On Windows, press `Win + X` and choose `Windows PowerShell`.
2. Copy and paste this command, then press Enter:

```powershell
irm https://get.classpad.dev/ | iex
```

3. Follow the installer steps below.

Not on Windows or having issues? [Join our Discord server](https://discord.gg/knpcNJTzpd) for help.

## Installer steps

### 1. Start the update

Click `Update to HollyHock V3`.

![Click Update to HollyHock V3](/wiki/img/install/v3/step1.png)

### 2. Download the updater

Wait while the installer downloads and prepares the updater. When the `Continue` button is ready, click it.

![Wait for the updater to download](/wiki/img/install/v3/step2.png)

### 3. Plug in the calculator

Plug your calculator into your computer with USB. If the calculator asks you to pick a USB connection mode, do not choose one. Click `Cancel` on the calculator popup, then click `Continue` in the installer.

![Plug in your calculator](/wiki/img/install/v3/step3.png)

### 4. Prepare the calculator

Power off the calculator, then hold `EXP`, `^`, and `Clear`.

While holding these three keys, press the reset button on the back of the calculator for a moment. Keep holding `EXP`, `^`, and `Clear` until the calculator shows `OS ERROR`.

When `OS ERROR` is shown on the calculator, click `Launch the Update`.

![Prepare the calculator for update mode](/wiki/img/install/v3/step4.png)

### 5. Confirm the updater

The official updater window will open. It repeats the same connection and key combo steps. When your calculator shows `OS ERROR`, click `OK`.

The update usually takes about 10 minutes. Do not unplug the calculator while it is updating.

![Confirm the updater instructions](/wiki/img/install/v3/step5.png)

### 6. Finish

When the installer says your calculator is ready, click `Finish`. You can now start your calculator and check that the HollyHock Launcher is available.

![Finish the installer](/wiki/img/install/v3/step6.png)

## Copy files to your calculator

When the installer is done, plug your calculator back in as USB storage.

First, download the HollyHock launcher file:

1. Open the [first run steps](https://classpaddev.github.io/first-run/#steps).
2. Download `run.bin`.
3. Keep the file named `run.bin`.

Then transfer the files to your ClassPad II:

1. Connect your ClassPad II to your computer with USB.
2. Copy `run.bin` to the root directory of your calculator storage.
3. Copy any `.hh3` programs, games, and Python apps you want to run to the root directory too.
4. Safely eject your calculator from your computer.

## Launch HollyHock

1. Go to the ClassPad home screen.
2. Open `System`.
3. Open the top left `System` menu.
4. Choose `Hollyhock Launcher`.

![Open System on the ClassPad](https://classpad.dev/img/step1_system.png)

![Open Hollyhock Launcher from the System menu](https://classpad.dev/img/step2_launcher.png)

## Run your app

In the launcher, pick the app you want to run from the list and click `Run`.

The launcher is a bit slow, so be sure to press for a good second.

![Pick your app in the Hollyhock Launcher](https://classpad.dev/img/step3_yal.png)

Your app should now run.

![CPBoy running on the ClassPad](https://classpad.dev/img/step4_run.png)

## Troubleshooting

### Calculator freezes

If your calculator freezes when launching HollyHock, check that `run.bin` was copied correctly to the root directory. Try downloading `run.bin` again and copying it one more time.

### Nothing displays in the launcher

If no programs appear in the launcher, check that you copied `.hh3` programs or games to the root directory of your calculator. The launcher needs these files to show options.

### File transfer issues

If you have trouble transferring files, make sure your calculator is properly connected and recognized by your computer. Try using a different USB cable if needed.
